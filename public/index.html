How It Works

Maximum Heart Rate Calculation

Your estimated maximum heart rate is calculated using 220 minus your age. For example, someone who is 30 years old would have an estimated maximum heart rate of 190 BPM.

Exercise Heart Rate Zones

Moderate Exercise (50–70%): This range represents moderate-intensity exercise based on your estimated maximum heart rate.

Vigorous Exercise (70–85%): This range represents higher-intensity exercise based on your estimated maximum heart rate.

Current Heart Rate

You can optionally enter your current heart rate in beats per minute (BPM). The calculator compares your entered reading with your estimated exercise zones.

Heart Rate Classification

Based on the entered heart rate, the calculator can classify your reading as below the estimated exercise zones, moderate intensity, vigorous intensity, or above the estimated exercise zones.

Heart Rate Feedback

The calculator provides feedback based on your classification to help explain how your current reading compares with the estimated exercise ranges.

This classification is not a medical assessment of whether your heart rate is normal or abnormal.

Calculation History

Successful calculations are automatically saved using your browser's local storage. You can view previous calculations in the Saved Calculation History section.

To remove saved calculations, click Clear Saved History and confirm the deletion.

Copy Results

After completing a calculation, click Copy Results to copy your maximum heart rate, exercise zones, and available classification information to your clipboard.

These calculations are estimates and are not medical advice.
